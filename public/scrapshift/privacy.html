Scrapshift privacy policy

Effective date: 10-10-2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Scrapshift ("the game"), made by DashPanda ("we"), collects and how it is used.

Information the game stores on your device

Your progress (coins, gems, level, upgrades, stars and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive this information. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google: your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address (used to estimate a rough location), how you interact with ads and the app (such as launches and taps), and diagnostic information (such as crashes and performance).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice from the game's settings. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the menu name varies by phone).

Learn more in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics

To understand how the game is played and fix problems, the game may send anonymous usage events (for example: a match started or ended, an upgrade bought, an ad watched) and crash reports through Google Analytics for Firebase. These events are linked to a random app-instance ID, not to your name or email.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, but never your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Ad consent: change it in the game's settings (where consent is required).
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your Android settings.
  • All game data: uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
