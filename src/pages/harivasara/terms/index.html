1. The app

The app shows Ekadashi dates, tithi timings and Parana (fast-breaking) windows for selected Indian cities, sends optional reminders, and provides the traditional Mahatmya (glory) of each Ekadashi to read. It's free, has no ads and requires no account.

2. Accuracy of dates and timings

  • Ekadashi dates and Parana times are based on published Gaudiya Vaishnava calendar calculations for each listed city. They're included in the app and updated with new app versions.
  • Observances can differ between traditions, sampradayas, temples and locations. Timings are given in Indian Standard Time and may not be accurate for places other than the listed cities.
  • I try hard to keep the data correct but can't guarantee it's free of errors. For important observances, please confirm with your local temple, guru or the calendar you follow.
