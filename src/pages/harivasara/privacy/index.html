Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

In short: Harivasara does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Everything you set in the app is stored only on your own device.

This policy explains how the Harivasara mobile app (“the app”), published by Subinay Panda (“I”, “me”), handles information. It applies to the app on Android and iOS and to this website.

1. Information I collect

None. The app has no sign-up or login, and it does not send any information about you or your device to me or to anyone else. I have no server that receives data from the app.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves a few settings in its private storage on your phone. This data never leaves your device, and I cannot see it.

WhatWhy
Language, city and theme (light, dark or system)To show the app in your language and calculate Ekadashi and Parana times for your city.
Reminder settings and reminder timeTo schedule the reminders and Parana alarms you turn on.
A list of reminders the app has scheduled or shownTo show your notification history inside the app.
Whether you've finished the first-run setupSo the setup screens are shown only once.

Your city is picked by you from a list. The app does not use GPS or your location.

3. Permissions

The app asks only for what it needs for reminders. You can refuse or revoke any of these in your phone's settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

  • Notifications: to show Ekadashi reminders and the Parana alarm. It's only requested when you turn reminders on.
  • Alarms & reminders (Android exact alarms): so the Parana alarm rings at the exact start of the Parana window. Without it, reminders may arrive a few minutes late.
  • Run at startup (Android): to restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Do Not Disturb override (optional, Android): only if you turn it on for the Parana alarm channel in system settings.

The app doesn't access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or location.

4. Third parties

  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party tracking.
  • The app is distributed through Google Play (and, if available, the Apple App Store). These stores may collect information under their own privacy policies when you download or update the app.
  • The “Rate this app” and “Share” buttons open the Play Store or your phone's share menu. What happens there is governed by those apps.
  • This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when you visit. The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

5. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

6. Deleting your data

All app data is on your device. To delete it, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Apps → Harivasara → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the app. There is no account and nothing to delete on my side.

7. Security

Your settings are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. No data is sent over the internet, so none can be intercepted.

8. Your rights

Because I don't collect or process your personal data, there's no personal data held by me to access, correct or erase. This includes under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If you have any question about this, contact me using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding crash reporting, I'll update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
