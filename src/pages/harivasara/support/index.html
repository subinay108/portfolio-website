Reminders & alarms

I'm not getting any reminders
  • In the app, open Settings and make sure Enable Notifications is on, along with the reminders you want (Upcoming Ekadashi, Parana Time).
  • Check that notifications are allowed for Harivasara in your phone's settings.
  • Battery savers can block reminders. On Android, go to Settings → Apps → Harivasara → Battery and choose Unrestricted. On Xiaomi, Redmi, Oppo, Vivo and Realme phones, also turn on Autostart for the app.
  • Open the app once after restarting your phone or updating the app. It reschedules everything when it opens.
The Parana alarm rings late

Android needs the Alarms & reminders permission to ring at the exact minute. In Settings, turn on Parana Alarm; if exact alarms are off, the app shows a note you can tap to allow them. Without this permission, Android may deliver the alarm a few minutes late.

The alarm is silent during Do Not Disturb

Apps can't override Do Not Disturb on their own. In Settings, tap the note under Parana Alarm, then turn on Override Do Not Disturb for the Parana alarm on the screen that opens.

Dates & timings

Which cities are supported?

Kolkata, New Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Ujjain, Varanasi, Guwahati and Itanagar. Choose the one nearest to you in Settings → Locate Nearby City. Want another city? Email me. Requests help decide which to add next.

A date differs from my temple's calendar

Ekadashi and Parana are calculated from sunrise and tithi timings for each city, and traditions sometimes observe a different day. Timings are in Indian Standard Time. If you think a date is wrong for your city, please email me with the details; otherwise, follow your temple or guru.

The app says “No upcoming Ekadashi dates”

The calendar data is included in each app version. When it runs out, update Harivasara from Google Play to get the next year's dates.

App

How do I change the language or theme?

Open Settings and choose Language (English, हिन्दी, বাংলা) or Appearance (light, dark or follow the system).

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored only on your phone, and there's no account. Clear the app's storage (Android: Settings → Apps → Harivasara → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the app. See the Privacy Policy.

Does the app cost anything or show ads?

No. Harivasara is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
